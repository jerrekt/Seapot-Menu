Usage & Rules
Take Only What You'll Finish — Please only grab the amount of food you intend to eat to help reduce food wastage.
Conveyor Belt Ordering — If you have specific requests not available on the belt, order from your server.
Hygiene — Inform a staff member if you accidentally drop or grab an unwanted item. DO NOT PUT ITEMS BACK ON THE CONVEYOR BELT. Staff will dispose of it and clear empty plates.
Allergies — Inform our staff of any allergies or dietary restrictions. We can assist you in selecting appropriate items.
Report Issues — Report any issues with the conveyor belt or food items to a staff member.
90-Minute AYCE Limit — All guests at a table must order the same selection (Hot Pot, BBQ, or Both).
Leftovers — Leftover food incurs a 20% charge based on the entire bill. Leftover food is not available for to-go.
No outside drinks or food allowed.
Last call is 30 minutes before closing time.
Thank you for dining at Seapot. Enjoy your meal!
